Privacy notice

What we collect through this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form we collect your name, company name (optional), phone number, work email, the service you are interested in, and your message.

Job applications

If you use the Careers page, we collect your name, phone, email, city, type of work, experience and any message you add. We use these details to consider you for job opportunities and to contact you about them.

Why we collect it

We use these details to respond to your enquiry about manpower, staffing and HR services, or to consider a job application and contact you about suitable opportunities. We do not sell your details.

Who processes it

The form is delivered to the KRIYOM team by Web3Forms, a third-party form service. Your details pass through their systems on the way to us.

Cookies, analytics and storage

Essential storage. The site stores a session flag so the welcome animation plays only once per visit, and a note of your cookie choice. These are needed for the site to work as intended and are always on.

Analytics (only if you accept). If you click “Accept analytics”, we use Google Analytics 4. It sets cookies whose names start with _ga and collects information such as the pages you view, how long you stay, your approximate location (city level), device and browser type, and how you reached the site. We use this only to understand and improve the website. It is not used to identify you personally and is not combined with your enquiry details.

Google acts as a processor for this data. If you click “Decline”, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics data is sent.

Change your mind. You can delete cookies and other browser storage in your browser settings. If analytics consent is enabled on this site, the consent notice provides the available choice controls.

Fonts. The site loads its font (Montserrat) from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when a page opens.

Your choices
